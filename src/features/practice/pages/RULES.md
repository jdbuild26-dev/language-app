# Main Practice rules

These pages implement Main Practice. Vocabulary Practice lives in `src/features/vocabulary/pages/` and has a separate behavior contract. Identify the exercise from its route and page before editing. A request naming an exercise or showing its screen authorizes changes to that exercise; do not require the user to say “Main Practice.”

## Screenshot changes

Main Practice text option cards use visible borders with their existing selected and correct/wrong colours. Remove the shadow-only treatment and layered shadows. Keep styles scoped to Main Practice, preserve semantic backgrounds and icons, and retain keyboard focus outlines and radio-circle outlines.

After submission, keep unselected answer options readable at full opacity. Keep their translation buttons clearly visible and usable; use color and borders to distinguish correct and incorrect answers without fading the remaining choices.

Complete the Passage follows the same submitted-option visibility rule. Its per-option translation icon uses the option's feedback color for correct and selected incorrect answers, and a clear blue for neutral answers.

Reading Comprehension initially shows each question and all its answer options in the learning language, including for beginner levels. Keep that language through submission. After submission, its translate button switches the entire question-and-options bundle to the known language and back.

Listen and Select option cards use 2px borders with neutral resting, indigo selected, green correct and red incorrect states. Do not use layered card shadows. Keep selection indicators in their fixed positions.

Listen and Select keeps its selection indicator in the same leftmost 24px slot before and after submission, with the speaker immediately after it. Submitted check/cross icons replace the indicator in that slot. Retain matching card padding and gaps so replay, feedback and translation do not move the indicator horizontally.

Main Practice scroll areas share slim grey rounded thumbs, transparent tracks, and darker hover feedback in light and dark mode. Keep the shared rules scoped to `data-main-practice`; portalled exercise dialogs opt in with `data-main-practice-scroll`. Include panels, answer textareas, dropdowns, conversation lists and reports. Retain stable scrollbar gutters where already reserved. Chromium uses explicit 6px scrollbar styling with both native scrollbar properties reset; other browsers use thin native scrollbars. Keep this treatment shared rather than adding page-specific scrollbar overrides.

All Main Practice passage bodies use justified alignment, including original, translated, corrected and completed passages. Keep the final line naturally aligned to the text's start. Use `practice-passage-content` for passage bodies; `practice-reading-passage-text` includes the same alignment. Apply the alignment to passage text rather than whole panels so headings, questions, dropdowns and answer controls retain their own alignment.

Main Practice printable typing outside editable controls automatically focuses the current editable answer field and inserts the first character at its saved caret/selection. Prefer the last focused answer field, otherwise the first empty editable field. Preserve character limits, shortcuts, composition, other editable controls, dialogs and Space activation of buttons/links. Do not redirect typing when no visible editable answer remains, including submitted/disabled screens. Scope this behavior to Main Practice routes and remove its listeners on exit; Grammar and Vocabulary Practice keep their existing behavior.

Main Practice learner-answer text inputs and textareas that have placeholders use `|` instead of written prompts. It blinks while the field is empty and editable; focus hides it in favor of the native caret. Entered text naturally removes the placeholder. Submitted read-only and disabled fields never show the placeholder, including unanswered fields and reduced-motion mode. Reduced-motion users see a steady line only on empty, unfocused, editable fields. Scope new cursor-state rules to Main Practice; Grammar and Vocabulary Practice retain their current behavior. Keep dropdown placeholders, existing blank-cell layouts, accessible labels, answer values and focus behavior intact.

Listening Passage's question and choices start in the same learning language, even at beginner levels. Before submission, each question's translation button switches only that question's text; its options stay in the learning language. Its passage translation action only shows or hides the passage translation and never changes question or option languages. After submission, per-question toggles independently switch that question and its options as one complete bundle. Timer expiry submits unanswered items as incorrect rather than leaving an expired, unsubmitted screen. Listen and Type replays do not reset the exercise timer. Listening Continue/Finish stops the previous audio; What do you hear? uses the same light-idle/solid-playing slow/normal distinction as Listen and Type.

Listening Reorder's audio controls use the same playback distinction as Listen and Type: both light blue when idle; only the playing row's active turtle (slow) or speaker (normal) is solid blue with a white icon and aria-pressed=true. Clear the visual highlight when playback ends or changes rows, and retain this distinction after submission.

Listening Passage Questions shuffles answer options once when loaded, using the same index permutation for the original, French, and English option arrays and remapping correctIndex. Keep this order stable through selections, playback, translation, and submission. Never shuffle the language arrays independently or identify the correct answer by label text, which can repeat.

Listening Passage Questions matches Reading Comprehension typography: Noto Sans questions and options at 20px below lg and 21px at lg, weight 400, including translated and submitted states. Passage titles stay 22/24px at weight 500. Question text uses the shared content role with relaxed leading, rather than the larger question-heading role.

What do you hear? places its correct-answer bubble translation button to the left of the bubbles in both French and English states.

Listen and Type's turtle and speaker are both light blue when idle. Highlight only the currently playing control in solid blue with a white icon: turtle for slow playback and speaker for normal playback. Return both to idle styling when playback ends; switching controls changes the playback mode immediately. Preserve explicit playback without autoplay.

Listen and Type keeps exact-answer scoring. Its character counter and textarea limit use a positive `Character Limit` value from the exercise when present; older exercises without that value retain the existing 100-character minimum. The special-character dropdown selects a language, then shows buttons for that language's characters. It defaults to the current learning language and inserts clicked characters at the answer caret.

Listen and Type focuses its answer field when a question becomes ready. Printable typing elsewhere on the page returns focus to that field and preserves the first typed character and current caret position. Preserve shortcuts, editable fields, modal interactions, and Space activation of buttons; do not redirect typing after submission or completion. Center the revealed correct answer and its translation across the answer panel, with equal side space so the translate icon does not offset the sentence.

Listening Passage Questions omits the playback-status caption. Its turtle toggles slow mode: light rose is normal, solid rose is slow, with aria-pressed reflecting the state. Play, resume, seek, and replay retain the selected speed; replay starts from the beginning. Toggling speed during playback continues near the current word, while toggling when idle or paused does not start audio. Reset speed to normal for a new passage. Show a moving circular handle on its seekable waveform before and after submission.

Listening Reorder Sentences follows Reading Reorder's centered 1200px container, inset title divider, scrolling list, card spacing, and regular-weight sentence text. Keep turtle then speaker controls centered in each row and available after submission. Reserve the submitted French and stored English sentence space and translation-control space before submission so cards retain their dimensions. Keep learner tiles unchanged and retain the footer's correct answer for now. Disable translation when English content is absent, and reset the translation on Continue.

Listen and Type never autoplays on entry or question changes. Its speaker and turtle buttons start normal and slow playback explicitly. After submission, preserve the learner's exact text in the read-only answer field, colored green or red by the result. Show the correct sentence separately above the field; translation toggles that correct sentence only, leaving the learner's text intact.

Listening exercise audio starts only after the learner clicks its audio control or an answer option that explicitly plays audio. This applies to new turns in Listen Interactive and Listening Conversation as well as Listen and Type. Main Practice typing exercises use a language selector and special-character keys that default to the current learning language. Keep the selector in a stable position when its character set changes.

Audio cards with only a speaker icon play when clicked anywhere on the card; the speaker is decorative. Cards with separate speaker and turtle controls retain explicit normal-speed and slow-speed buttons. Listen and Select selects and plays the card before submission, and replays without changing the answer after submission. Submit must not interrupt playback. Its feedback bar uses the compact layout and omits the repeated correct answer because the submitted options already display it.

All passage translation sections (Reading Comprehension, Diagram Labelling, Complete the Passage, Fill in the Blanks Passage, and Listening Passage) show the stored translated title above the translated body, falling back to "Passage" when a translated title is absent. Use weight 500 and retain the original title size. Reserve scrollbar space on the actual scroll container, including nested custom-scrollbar panels, before translation expands it so passage width and wrapping do not shift.

Main Practice content/passage headings and panel instructions that previously used bold weight 700 use weight 500, including their translated states. Preserve every heading's existing font size, line height, and layout. Main exercise headers in the shared chrome remain at their original weight 700. Keep existing regular-weight questions, answer text, number badges, error headings, completion screens, and report headings unchanged.

Translation actions prefer stored bilingual text without a request or loading state, including identical source/target words. This applies to every Main Practice translation action, including shared answer rows and conversation/interactive prompts. Preserve bilingual exchange fields during normalization and resolve the counterpart of the displayed prompt, question or speaker text; reset the translation on both turn and conversation changes. Reorder passage titles and Highlight questions pass their stored counterparts to the shared translation hook; Highlight Text uses its already selected opposite-language question directly. Only request a translation when the counterpart is absent, using the actual displayed source language. Preserve existing typography and layout while toggling.

For answer feedback across exercise types, look up stored translations for both correct answers and selected distractors or options before requesting a translation. Keep the source and translated answer pair in one language at a time. Apply this when extending translation behavior to other exercises.

Fill in the Blanks Passage counts passage exercises in the header (for example, 1/5 for five passages), while scoring counts all blank answers across those passages. Its passage translation appears after submission and uses the stored translated passage with correct answers highlighted.

## Translate by Speaking

Use the supplied spoken-translation screenshots: plain centered source text at 24/30px weight 500, blue microphone changing to a square Stop icon, and a disabled replay control until an actual learner recording is available. Each recording has a fixed 60-second limit and stops automatically at that limit. After Submit, show the correct answer in green beneath the source, an inset horizontal rule, and the finalized recognized transcript colored by evaluation. Preserve replay and the shared Continue footer. Audio remains in browser memory only; release microphone tracks and revoke the recording URL on re-record, Next, exit, and unmount. Do not upload audio, synthesize replay from text, or show an AI report overlay for this exercise.

## Writing Fill in the Blanks

Use the segmented letter-box design from the supplied code.html with the user’s compact sizing adjustment: 28px-high cells, 23px wide for words with up to four cells and 21px for longer words, and 15px letters. Word groups have an 8px corner radius, 1px slate-300 border and a 2px slate bottom shadow. Supplied letters use a pale grey background and weight 500; editable cells are white with indigo text/caret and weight 600. Focus highlights the entire word group with an indigo border and translucent outer ring; the active cell has a pale lavender background. Select an editable cell's character on focus so it can be replaced directly. Include segmented cells in Main Practice type-to-answer behavior without adding placeholder marks to every cell. Retain submitted correctness colors, dark mode and horizontal access for words wider than the viewport. After submission or timer expiry, preserve learner cells and show a separate completed French passage beneath them with correct blank words in green. Hide accent keys after submission. Place the passage translation button at the top-right of the content header, separate from the centered passage title, only after submission when English is available. It switches the completed passage and title between French and English, leaving learner cells unchanged. Keep translated answer highlights green, using numbered markers or unambiguous whole-word matches of stored English answers; never substitute French for missing English mappings or guess ambiguous highlights. Keep a plain stored translation readable when highlights cannot be resolved. Reset translation, answers and focus on Continue. Omit the repeated correct-answer list from the feedback footer.
